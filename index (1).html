HOW TO PLAY

  • TAP, CLICK or SPACE switches the traffic light.
  • GREEN = cars drive. RED = walkers cross. Never mix them!
  • Hit a walker = -1 life. Safe crossings build COMBOS.
  • Waiting 5s = they beep at you. 10s = they quit and you lose a life.
  • 3 lives. Gold cars pay 5X. P pause · R restart · M music · N sound
